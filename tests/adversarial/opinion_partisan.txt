HEADLINE: The government's housing plan is a reckless failure

STANDFIRST: A sharp op-ed arguing the minister has mismanaged the crisis

BODY:
Let me be blunt: this housing plan is the worst policy I have seen in a decade, and
the minister responsible should be ashamed. The numbers do not lie. The department's
own figures show completions fell 12 percent last year, even as the minister
promised a boom. That is not spin; it is arithmetic.

I hold no brief for the opposition either — their plan was vague and underfunded.
But a government that publishes a target and then misses it by this margin has
forfeited the benefit of the doubt. Voters were promised 300,000 homes; they got a
press release. The public interest here is obvious: people cannot find somewhere to
live, and ministers keep congratulating themselves.

This is my opinion, argued from the public record. Readers are free to disagree.
