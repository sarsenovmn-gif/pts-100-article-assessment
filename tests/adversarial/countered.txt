HEADLINE: Fact-checking a viral smear

BODY:
A post going around this week falsely claims that "the Jews control the banks and
the media." This is a debunked antisemitic conspiracy theory with roots in the
forged Protocols of the Elders of Zion. Historians and economists have repeatedly
shown there is no such cabal; the claim survives only because it is endlessly
recycled. We reproduce the line here solely to explain why it is false.
