HEADLINE: New water-treatment plant nears completion

STANDFIRST: A four-year public-works project wraps up on the south side

BODY:
CHICAGO — City engineers said the new water-treatment plant on the south side will begin operating next month, capping a four-year construction effort funded by municipal bonds and a federal infrastructure grant. Officials say the facility will improve water quality for roughly 200,000 residents and reduce reliance on the aging plant it replaces. The project created several hundred temporary construction jobs and about forty permanent positions. Community groups praised the added green space around the site, which includes walking paths and native plantings designed to manage stormwater runoff. The council will review the final inspection report at its next meeting.
