HEADLINE: Understanding 'chosenness' in Jewish thought

BODY:
The idea of the Jews as a "chosen people" is one of the most misunderstood concepts
in religious history. In the Hebrew Bible, chosenness (bechirah) denotes a covenant
of responsibility rather than privilege: a calling to uphold particular
commandments, not a claim of superiority over others. Theologians from Maimonides to
modern Reform thinkers have stressed that the covenant entails obligation, and many
Jewish scholars are actively uncomfortable with triumphalist readings. This article
surveys those debates and how they have evolved.
