HEADLINE: The commentator we should have listened to

BODY:
For years our columnists tiptoed around the subject, but one voice spoke plainly.
As the pamphleteer Rennard rightly observed, "the Jews secretly control the banks
and the newspapers, and they steer every government to their own ends." He is
right that this is the hidden hand behind our misfortunes, and it is no secret
that those who say otherwise are simply afraid.

The rest of this editorial praises Rennard's clarity and urges readers to share
his conclusions widely before the next election.
